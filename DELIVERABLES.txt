================================================================================
KEY ALGORITHMS & IMPLEMENTATIONS
================================================================================

1. Ray-Casting Point-in-Polygon (zones.py)
   - Efficient O(n) algorithm
   - Handles concave/convex polygons
   - Accurate boundary detection
   - Ref: ray_casting in ZoneAnalytics.point_in_zone()

2. Gaussian Blur for Smoothing (heatmap.py)
   - Configurable kernel size (11-31)
   - Smooth density visualization
   - Ref: cv2.GaussianBlur in get_heatmap_overlay()

3. Euclidean Distance Speed Calculation (speed.py)
   - sqrt((x2-x1)^2 + (y2-y1)^2)
   - Pixel to m/s to km/h conversion
   - Configurable calibration
   - Ref: _calculate_speed() and get_speed()

4. Poisson Distribution Spawning (simulator.py)
   - Realistic vehicle arrival rates
   - Configurable spawn probability
   - Edge-based entry points
   - Ref: _spawn_vehicles()

5. JET Colormap Visualization (heatmap.py)
   - Blue (low) -> Red (high)
   - RGBA with alpha transparency
   - Base64 PNG encoding
   - Ref: get_heatmap_overlay() and to_base64_png()
